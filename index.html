Internal Validation Suite

Automated checks: textbook Black-Scholes reference values, European put-call parity, American ≥ European no-arbitrage ordering, binomial-tree convergence, and full round-trip IV recovery (price at a known σ → invert → compare). These verify numerical correctness of the engine — they do not imply the models match live market prices.

Models & Conventions

American Pricing — Puts & Calls

Leisen-Reimer binomial tree (Peizer-Pratt method 2 inversion, odd steps) with Richardson extrapolation across two tree sizes, cancelling the leading discretization error. This is the primary engine — it handles early exercise exactly within the tree.

Closed-Form Cross-Check

Bjerksund-Stensland 2002 two-boundary approximation for American calls, shown alongside the tree price. When the two disagree materially the tree is authoritative and the toolkit flags it.

European Pricing

Black-Scholes-Merton with continuous dividend yield. Normal CDF via Cody (1969) rational minimax (~15 significant digits); bivariate normal via Drezner-Genz Gauss-Legendre quadrature.

IV Root-Finding

European seed (analytic-vega Newton), then damped Newton on the American tree price with model-consistent numerical vega, with a bracketing Brent failsafe. Residual tolerance 10⁻⁸ on price.

Dividends

Continuous yield q, or a single discrete cash dividend via the escrowed method: spot is reduced by the present value of the dividend when the ex-date precedes expiry.

Conventions

T = calendar days / 365 (ACT/365). Rates & yields are annualized, continuously compounded. Theta is per calendar day; vega & rho are per 1 percentage point. Prices are per share — multiply by 100 for a standard US equity contract.
